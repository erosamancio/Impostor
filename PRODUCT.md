# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Eros and his girlfriend, who play the social word game "impostor" together, sometimes with friends. They play in person, at home or out, passing one phone from hand to hand. Most sessions are two people (Modo dupla); with friends, three or more (modo clássico).

## Product Purpose

A free web version of the impostor word game with no ads, no login and every theme unlocked. Everyone gets the same secret word except the impostor, who gives hints to blend in; the group discusses and votes. Success is a game that starts in seconds, keeps secrets safe while the phone changes hands, and offers enough good words that rounds stay fresh.

## Positioning

Built to replace a mobile app the couple found limited: few free themes, constant ads. This one keeps the essence of the genre, adds a two-player mode made for couples, and curates every word by hand so it is easy to hint and fair to guess.

## Operating Context

- One phone only, always. Pass-and-play: each player takes the phone, sees their secret, hands it on.
- Played face to face, often at night, in a living room or at a bar.
- Hints and discussion happen out loud; the phone paces turns, keeps the order, collects votes and reveals results.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Modes: Clássico (3 to 20 players, one impostor) and Dupla (2 players, same or similar word, bet "iguais" or "diferentes").
- 7 curated themes in Brazilian Portuguese: Comidas e bebidas, Países, Lugares do Brasil, Lugares da cidade, Animais, Profissões, Esportes. Words carry a difficulty and sister words.
- Secret reveal by press-and-hold; secret vote by passing the phone; impostor's last chance to guess the word; running score for the night.
- No repetition of words until a theme is exhausted; everything stored on the device.
- Static site on GitHub Pages; React + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind.
- Portuguese (pt-BR) only.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Impostor.
- Original design: must not copy the layout, characters or look of any existing impostor app.
- Commits in the repository are authored by Eros, with no co-author or tool attribution.

## Evidence on Hand

No testimonials, metrics or third-party assets. Word lists in `src/temas/` are the real content.

## Product Principles

1. Quality over quantity: fewer themes, every word good to play.
2. Secrets are sacred: nothing secret shows unless the right person is holding the phone.
3. Seconds to start: from opening the site to the first secret in under 30 seconds.
4. Free and quiet: no ads, no accounts, no network needed once loaded.
