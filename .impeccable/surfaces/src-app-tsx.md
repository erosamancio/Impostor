# Surface: Impostor app (all screens)

Mode: Operate. Pass-and-play party game on one phone, played at night face to face. Jobs: set up a game in seconds, reveal secrets safely, pace hint turns, collect votes, show results and the night's score.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The phone is a sealed envelope passed around the table. Every secret lives under a bank-envelope security tint that only lifts while the right person presses it. Refuses the category default of flat neon cards and emoji confetti.

OWN-WORLD: Envelope-interior ink blue ground (#0f1a33) with paper-blue surfaces, cool paper text, security-tint pattern (fine diagonal hatching plus repeated tiny "IMPOSTOR" lettering in two blues), postage-yellow primary actions, airmail red for the impostor, postal green for success. Bricolage Grotesque throughout, tabular numerals for scores. Drawn line icons (Lucide), soft offset shadows, 14px corners, perforated-stamp edge on the theme tiles.

STORY: Players understand at a glance whose turn it is, trust that nobody else can see their word, and feel the reveal and the verdict as moments: a postmark stamp lands on the result.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: a large sealed envelope (flap, tint visible, a stamp corner with the game mark) fills the upper half; "Impostor" wordmark under it; primary "Novo jogo" and "Jogar de novo" pinned to the bottom bar; night score below the fold when it exists.

FORM: Envelope de segurança, grounded candidate 5 of 7 (boteco, truco night, painted shop lettering, bakery queue ticket, security envelope, detective noir, carnival bloco); seed key fa786378 (degraded roll, no challengers).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
